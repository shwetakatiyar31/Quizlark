How accounts work

Make an account with any email address, personal, work or school. We send a link to that email, and once you click it your account is verified.

Your coins, critters, accessories, banners, stats and your own question sets save to your account, and load on any computer or phone where you log in.

If you played as a guest first, that progress moves into your account the first time you log in.
